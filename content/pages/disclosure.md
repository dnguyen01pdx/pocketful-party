Pocketful Party is free to read and the printables are free to download. To keep it running, some posts link to paid products:

- **Our own shop.** Links to Pocketful Studio go to our own Etsy shop. If you buy something there, we earn the sale.
- **Affiliate links.** Some posts may include affiliate links (for example, to party supplies). If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Posts with affiliate links say so near the top, and we only link to things that fit the game in the post.
- **Ads.** The site may show ads in the future. Ads never change what we write.

We don't accept payment to review or recommend a product.
