Med Mapped is free to read and the printables are free to download. To keep it running, some posts link to paid products:

- **My own shop.** Links to Med Mapped on Etsy go to my own Etsy shop, where I sell application tracker spreadsheets and a few party printables. If you buy something there, I earn the sale.
- **Affiliate links.** Some posts may include affiliate links in the future (for example, to study tools). If you buy through one, I may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Posts with affiliate links will say so near the top, and I'll only link to things I'd actually recommend.
- **Ads.** The site may show ads in the future. Ads never change what I write.

Nobody pays me to review or recommend a product.
